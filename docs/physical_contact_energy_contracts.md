# Bounded axial contact energy qualification

The optional contact source gate qualifies contained annotated candidates from an
exact validated Ball donor export and a current Particle backend identity. It
never modifies either owner, the immutable packet, installed apps or defaults.

```sh
python3 -B tests/integration/run_contact_energy_contracts.py \
  --compiler /path/to/fisics --particle-root /path/to/particle-main-edit \
  --donor-root /path/to/validated/contact-donor \
  --backend-build /path/to/qualified/backend --out /tmp/contact-contracts-new
```

The backend builder/importer must establish committed-parent, generated-validator,
shared-header and candidate manifest provenance first. The gate validates the
export, current engine inputs and exact before/candidate transformation hashes.
Clang prepares actual translation units; physical attributes remain for selected
fisiCs checking. Five helper bodies cover translational kinetic energy, scalar
momentum change/normal momentum transfer, observed energy loss and balance. They
establish15 checked obligations with one explicit half-literal assumption and no
declared-boundary permission. The sphere spin term has dimensional coverage only.
Four raw enclosing functions deliberately refuse physical-strict coverage.

Scalar momentum transfer has kg m/s dimensions. Effective-mass derivation, raw
particle/matrix fields, geometry, vector frames and contact/constitutive meaning
remain declared boundaries. Generic energy is not inferred heat or a kinetic
energy-form conversion. Existing finite operations are reused; no compiler rule,
public C layout, numerical equation, guard, solver budget or version is changed.
The policy/catalog snapshots in the fixture are qualification inputs, not a new
shared runtime library or adoption of the donor into canonical Ball.

The selected runtime envelope is three X-axis spheres, radius0.125m, mass0.25..4kg,
|vx|<=2m/s, equal y/z, zero transverse velocity/spin/gravity, no planes/fields and
restitution/friction0. It retains1ms outer steps,64 sweeps and existing group/TOI
limits. Passing the seven frozen fixtures does not prove every admitted state.
General tumbling/off-axis/full-inertia contact and the known failed external
angular-momentum profile remain excluded.

Particle observes group loss Kbefore-Kafter. Its existing negative roundoff floor
is -1e-10*max(1,Kbefore); accepted negative roundoff clips to zero. Momentum has
its existing1e-10 scaled guard. The outer balance guard is1e-10 scaled. These
refusal gates are distinct from independent physical acceptance. There is no
new heat-transfer or material-plasticity law.

Separate runtime evidence must join actual objects to the real Particle host,
exercise both C ABI directions, preserve the immutable donor, and pass the
independent sticky-cluster trajectory/momentum/energy-plus-loss oracle and atomic
refusal/retry tests. `trace.py` independently checks accepted tick/group coverage,
K, impulse/momentum transfer, group loss, event times and outer-tick loss sums for
the seven fixed cases. Qualification-only staged rows are emitted after whole-tick
success; existing v10 artifacts and solver-free reader meanings stay unchanged.
Trace consistency and source seals do not authenticate data or replace the
independent physical oracle. Compiler acceptance alone establishes no mechanics.

No canonical/installed/default/compiler cutover follows from this gate. Retain
exact prepared inputs, object/link receipts and separate reference/cost evidence
when reporting a completed bounded qualification.
